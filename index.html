01 / About

Technology becomes useful when it helps people make better decisions.

Research grounded in practice.

I am a Professor and Head of Discipline in Software Systems at the University of Technology Sydney, and Director of the DigiSAS Research and Innovation Lab for Software and Societal Systems.

Across more than 25 years in IT, I have worked across strategy, adaptive intelligent enterprise architecture, AI systems architecture, agile software development, product engineering and innovation. My experience spans higher education, government, consulting and other sectors.

I have led research and commercial engagements, developed teams, and trained and coached architects, software engineers and data professionals. My work brings research and industry together to turn complex ideas into useful methods, architectures and working systems.

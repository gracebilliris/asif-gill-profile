Asif Gill

Professor, researcher and technology leader working across human-centred AI, adaptive enterprise architecture and software systems.

I am Head of Discipline, Software Systems at the University of Technology Sydney and Director of the DigiSAS Research and Innovation Lab for Software and Societal Systems.

My work connects research with practice. Over more than 25 years in IT, I have worked across strategy, enterprise and AI systems architecture, agile software development, product engineering and innovation. I have led research and commercial engagements across higher education, government, consulting and industry.

I develop practical ways to think, architect, build and evaluate intelligent systems that augment human decision-making and create value for organisations, workforces and society.

Current work

  • Human-centred AI systems Observable, resilient and responsible architectures for augmented intelligence.
  • Adaptive enterprise architecture Connecting strategy, information, capabilities and technology in changing organisations.
  • Software and societal systems Applied research for environmental, economic, health and social wellbeing.

Selected work

Books Four books on human-centred AI, enterprise architecture and strategy Publications A searchable archive of 225 research outputs from 2006–2026 Writing Notes on AI systems, architecture and digital transformation

Industry and service

I work with industry and government on AI transformation, operating models, workforce capability and enterprise-scale system design. Recent engagements include an Agentic AI workshop with Amazon Web Services and a joint keynote on the AI trust gap at CIO 50 Leadership Live Australia.

My professional service includes fellowship of the Australian Computer Society, senior membership of IEEE, Standards Australia IT-015, IFIP Working Group 8.1 and editorial work for IEEE Transactions on Technology and Society.

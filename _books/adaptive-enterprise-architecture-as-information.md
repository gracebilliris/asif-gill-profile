Enterprise architecture is often treated as a fixed set of diagrams and
documents. This book instead frames architecture as information that can be
created, connected and used throughout an organisation.

The approach helps enterprises maintain coherence while their strategies,
capabilities, information and technologies change. It connects architectural
practice with the needs of intelligent enterprises operating in dynamic digital
ecosystems.

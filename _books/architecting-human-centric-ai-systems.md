The book examines how human and artificial intelligence can be architected as
an integrated system. It focuses on augmented intelligence: using AI to extend
human capability while retaining meaningful judgement, accountability and
control.

Its architecture perspective connects perception, thinking and action with the
enterprise foundations needed for secure, observable and resilient AI systems.
The work is intended for architects, technology leaders, researchers and teams
moving from isolated AI demonstrations to operational capability.

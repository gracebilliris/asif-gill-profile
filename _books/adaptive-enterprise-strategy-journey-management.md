This book presents enterprise strategy as an ongoing journey rather than a
one-time planning exercise. It addresses the gap between strategic intent and
execution by focusing on how organisations sense change, make decisions and
adapt their direction.

The journey-management perspective is designed for leaders and practitioners
who need to coordinate business and technology change without assuming that
plans, conditions or priorities will remain fixed.

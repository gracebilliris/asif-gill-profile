Cloud adoption is an organisational architecture challenge as much as a
technology decision. This book develops an adaptive approach that connects
cloud services with enterprise capabilities, information and operating needs.

It offers a structured perspective for architects and technology leaders
working across business and IT boundaries in environments where services and
requirements continue to evolve.

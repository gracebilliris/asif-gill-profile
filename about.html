About

I am a professor, researcher and technology leader with more than 25 years of experience across academia and industry.

I am Head of Discipline, Software Systems at the University of Technology Sydney and Director of the DigiSAS Research and Innovation Lab for Software and Societal Systems.

My work spans strategy, adaptive intelligent enterprise architecture, AI systems architecture, agile software development, product engineering and innovation. I have led research and commercial engagements across higher education, government, consulting and industry, and have trained and coached architects, software engineers and data professionals.

My research focuses on how organisations can think, architect, build and evaluate intelligent systems that augment human decision-making. This includes observable and resilient Agentic AI, adaptive enterprise architecture, trustworthy data ecosystems and software systems designed for societal value.

I hold a PhD and MSc in Computing and a Master of Business. I am a Fellow of the Australian Computer Society and a senior member of IEEE. My professional service includes Standards Australia IT-015, IFIP Working Group 8.1 and editorial work for IEEE Transactions on Technology and Society.

Industry engagement

I work with industry and government on AI transformation, operating models, workforce capability and enterprise-scale system design. Recent engagements include an Agentic AI workshop with Amazon Web Services and a joint keynote on the AI trust gap at CIO 50 Leadership Live Australia.

Research areas

  • Human-centred and augmented intelligence
  • Agentic AI systems architecture
  • Adaptive enterprise architecture and strategy
  • Agile software and product engineering
  • Data ecosystems, observability and trust
